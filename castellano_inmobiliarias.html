Your Latin American Spanish is your strongest asset. The differences below are largely phonological and lexical — not grammatical. Spanish professionals will understand you perfectly from day one. Your goal is to understand them and adapt your register to build professional rapport.
La distinción — The key pronunciation difference
In mainland Spain (outside southern Andalucía and the Canary Islands), the letters c (before e or i) and z are pronounced like the English "th" in think. This is called la distinción. It is not a lisp — it is a completely distinct phoneme. The myth that "all Spaniards lisp" confuses this with ceceo, a separate regional phenomenon.
Latin American
precio → "pre-SYO"
notario → "no-TA-ryo"
doce → "DO-se"
escritura → "es-kri-TU-ra"
Castilian (Spain)
precio → "pre-THYO"
notario → same (no c/z)
doce → "DO-the"
escritura → same (no c/z)
Practice words with c/z: precio, plazo, notario, hipoteca, escritura, tasación, avanzado, terraza, plaza
Vosotros — The second-person plural you must master
Latin American Spanish uses ustedes for all plural "you" contexts (formal and informal). In Spain, vosotros/vosotras is the informal plural, and ustedes is reserved for formal or distant contexts. In agency offices and informal professional settings you will hear vosotros constantly.
Latin American
¿Ustedes quieren ver el piso?
¿Cuándo vienen ustedes?
Cuando lleguen...
Castilian
¿Vosotros queréis ver el piso?
¿Cuándo venís vosotros?
Cuando lleguéis...
Key vosotros endings: -áis / -éis / -ís (present); -asteis / -isteis (preterite); -ad / -ed / -id (imperatives: ¡venid!, ¡esperad!)

Register: how to address agents and lawyers
On first contact — always use usted. This is respectful and expected in all professional settings in Spain, just as in Latin America.
Agents in Spain typically switch to tú more quickly than in Latin America — once they do, reciprocate.
With notaries and lawyers — remain in usted throughout the entire professional relationship.

Email openings: "Estimado/a señor/a García:" or "Me dirijo a usted en relación con la propiedad situada en..."
Email closings: "Quedo a su disposición para cualquier consulta." / "En espera de sus noticias, reciba un cordial saludo."
Critical advice: Always appoint an independent abogado — not the one recommended by the selling agency. Their duty is to represent you. Budget 1–2% of the purchase price. Never sign a contrato de arras without independent legal review.
El proceso de compra — The 11-step buying process
  1. 1
    Obtener el NIE — Get your Foreigner's ID Number
    The Número de Identidad de Extranjero is mandatory before signing any contract or paying taxes in Spain. Apply at the Spanish Consulate General in Melbourne — allow 4–6 weeks processing time. Alternatively apply at a Spanish comisaría (police station) with a Jefatura de Extranjería during your scouting trip.
    Do before your tripConsulado Melbourne
  2. 2
    Abrir cuenta bancaria española — Open a Spanish bank account
    Required to pay purchase costs, taxes, and ongoing expenses (IBI, community fees). Recommended banks for non-residents: Santander, BBVA, CaixaBank. Some allow non-resident account opening online with your NIE and passport. You will need this account before completing the purchase.
    Non-resident account
  3. 3
    Buscar propiedades — Property search
    Main portals: Idealista.es, Fotocasa.es, Habitaclia.es (Catalonia). Estate agents are called agencias inmobiliarias or simply inmobiliarias. Individual agents are agentes inmobiliarios. Commission is typically paid by the vendor in Spain — not the buyer.
    Online research phase
  4. 4
    Visitar propiedades — View properties
    Your May 2027 trip. Aim to arrange 8–15 viewings to calibrate your market understanding. Say: "Me gustaría concertar una visita para la propiedad de la calle..." Bring a notepad — agents will talk fast. The vocabulary and dialogues in this guide are your toolkit.
    May 2027 trip
  5. 5
    Hacer una oferta — Make an offer
    Offers can be verbal initially (oferta verbal). The agent takes your offer to the vendor (vendedor/propietario). Negotiating 5–10% below asking price is normal. A counter-offer is a contraoferta. Nothing is binding at this stage.
    Negotiate — 5–10% below asking is normal
  6. 6
    Contratar un abogado independiente — Appoint your lawyer
    Do this before signing anything. Your lawyer will obtain and review the nota simple, verify the IBI is paid, check for community debt, assess planning status, and draft or review the contrato de arras. Ask expat communities for recommendations.
    Critical — do before signing anything
  7. 7
    Firmar el contrato de arras — Sign the preliminary contract
    Typically 10% of the purchase price is paid as a deposit. Under Spanish law (arras penitenciales): if you (the buyer) withdraw, you forfeit the deposit. If the seller withdraws, they must return el doble — double the deposit. This makes the deal substantively binding.
    10% deposit — legally bindingLawyer review mandatory
  8. 8
    Gestionar la financiación — Arrange financing (if required)
    Non-residents can typically borrow up to 70% LTV from Spanish banks. The bank requires a tasación (independent valuation). Allow 4–6 weeks for mortgage approval. Subrogación means assuming the vendor's existing mortgage — sometimes advantageous if the rate is good.
    If applying for a mortgage
  9. 9
    Firmar la escritura ante notario — Sign the deed at the notary
    Both parties sign the escritura pública de compraventa at the notary's office. The balance of the purchase price is paid (typically by cheque bancario — a bank-certified cheque). The notary verifies identities and legality. The keys (llaves) are handed over at this moment.
    Completion day — keys handed over
  10. 10
    Pagar los impuestos — Pay taxes within 30 days
    For resale (segunda mano): pay ITP within 30 days of signing. For new build (obra nueva): pay IVA (10%) + AJD (1–1.5%). Your gestor or lawyer will handle the tax forms. Missing the 30-day deadline incurs penalties.
    30-day legal deadline
  11. 11
    Inscribir en el Registro de la Propiedad — Register the title
    Your lawyer/gestor files the escritura at the land registry (Registro de la Propiedad). Registration typically takes 2–8 weeks. You are the legal owner from the moment of signing, but registration makes your title public record — protecting you against third-party claims.
    Post-completion — handled by gestor

🏢 Escenario 1: Primera visita a la agencia inmobiliaria
Tú
Buenos días. Estoy interesada en comprar una propiedad en esta zona y me gustaría saber qué tienen disponible.
Good morning. I'm interested in buying a property in this area and I'd like to know what you have available.
Agente
Buenos días, claro. ¿Tiene algún tipo de propiedad en mente? ¿Piso, chalet, ático...?
Of course. Do you have any particular type of property in mind? Apartment, house, penthouse...?
Tú
Busco un piso de dos o tres dormitorios, preferiblemente con terraza y plaza de garaje incluida. El presupuesto máximo es de trescientos mil euros.
I'm looking for a 2 or 3 bedroom apartment, ideally with a terrace and parking space included. My maximum budget is €300,000.
Agente
Perfecto. ¿Tiene preferencia de zona o de orientación?
Great. Do you have a preferred neighbourhood or orientation?
Tú
Preferiblemente orientación sur o este, luminoso y exterior. Y con ascensor, si es posible.
Preferably south or east facing, bright and street-facing. And with a lift if possible.
Agente
Tenemos varios que se ajustan a lo que busca. ¿Cuándo podría venir a verlos?
We have several that match what you're looking for. When could you come to view them?
🚪 Escenario 2: Durante la visita al piso
Tú
¿Cuántos metros cuadrados tiene el piso exactamente?
How many square metres is the apartment exactly?
Agente
Tiene ochenta y cinco metros útiles y noventa y siete construidos.
It has 85 usable square metres and 97 built.
Tú
¿Y cuánto son los gastos de comunidad mensuales?
And how much are the monthly community fees?
Agente
Son ciento treinta euros al mes, que incluyen la limpieza del portal, el ascensor y el mantenimiento de la piscina.
It's €130 per month, which includes the entrance cleaning, lift and pool maintenance.
Tú
¿Cuándo se construyó el edificio? ¿Ha habido alguna reforma reciente?
When was the building built? Have there been any recent renovations?
Agente
El edificio es de los años noventa, pero se rehabilitó la fachada en dos mil diecinueve y se renovó el ascensor el año pasado.
The building is from the nineties, but the façade was renovated in 2019 and the lift was replaced last year.
Tú
¿Está libre de cargas? ¿Tiene hipoteca pendiente?
Is it free of charges? Is there an outstanding mortgage?
Agente
Sí, el propietario canceló la hipoteca el año pasado. Lo puede comprobar usted misma en la nota simple.
Yes, the owner paid off the mortgage last year. You can verify this yourself in the land registry extract.
Tú
¿Está el IBI al corriente de pago?
Is the annual council tax (IBI) up to date?
Agente
Sí, podemos facilitarle los últimos recibos.
Yes, we can provide you with the recent payment receipts.
💶 Escenario 3: Negociando el precio
Tú
El piso me interesa mucho, pero el precio me parece un poco elevado para la zona. ¿Hay margen para negociar?
I'm very interested in the apartment, but the price seems a little high for the area. Is there room to negotiate?
Agente
Podemos trasladar su oferta al propietario. ¿Qué cantidad tendría usted en mente?
We can pass your offer on to the owner. What figure did you have in mind?
Tú
Estaría dispuesta a ofrecer doscientos setenta y cinco mil euros, incluyendo la plaza de garaje.
I would be prepared to offer €275,000, including the parking space.
Agente
De acuerdo, lo consulto con el propietario y le llamo mañana. ¿Me puede dejar un número de contacto?
Understood. I'll check with the owner and call you tomorrow. Can you leave me a contact number?
Agente
[Al día siguiente] El propietario acepta bajar a doscientos ochenta y cinco mil, con la plaza incluida.
[The next day] The owner agrees to come down to €285,000, with the parking included.
Tú
Necesito consultarlo con mi abogado. Le confirmo antes de mañana por la tarde.
I need to consult my lawyer. I'll confirm before tomorrow afternoon.
⚖️ Escenario 4: Primera reunión con el abogado
Tú
Buenos días. Me gustaría contratar sus servicios para gestionar la compraventa de un piso en esta ciudad.
Good morning. I'd like to engage your services to manage the purchase of an apartment in this city.
Abogado
Por supuesto. Lo primero que haremos es solicitar la nota simple del Registro de la Propiedad para comprobar la titularidad y si existen cargas o gravámenes.
Of course. The first thing we'll do is request the land registry extract to verify ownership and check for any charges or encumbrances.
Tú
¿Qué impuestos tendré que pagar como compradora extranjera?
What taxes will I have to pay as a foreign buyer?
Abogado
Al tratarse de una vivienda de segunda mano, pagará el ITP. En esta comunidad autónoma es el ocho por ciento del precio escriturado. También habrá gastos de notaría y de registro.
Since it's a resale property, you'll pay ITP. In this region it's 8% of the declared purchase price. There will also be notary and registration fees.
Tú
¿Y cuánto son sus honorarios por llevar la operación completa?
And what are your fees for managing the whole transaction?
Abogado
Cobramos el uno por ciento del precio de compra más IVA, con un mínimo de mil quinientos euros. Incluye la revisión del contrato de arras, la escritura y la gestión del NIE si aún no lo tiene.
We charge 1% of the purchase price plus VAT, with a minimum fee of €1,500. This includes reviewing the arras contract, the deed, and NIE management if you don't already have it.
Tú
Perfecto. ¿Puede proporcionarme la nota simple de la propiedad que me interesa?
Perfect. Can you obtain the land registry extract for the property I'm interested in?
