🔍 Why This Journey Breaks Down
🔗
#01 No Integration
UniCRM and Banner are disconnected. Changes in one don't flow to the other.
✋
#02 Manual = Fragile
Staff must manually re-enter conditions in Banner. Delays and human error are inevitable.
📅
#03 Wrong Granularity
Banner's Term-level architecture can't support Part of Term conditions. A structural mismatch.
Ellucian Banner as configured is not fit for purpose for this institution's enrolment workflow.
